# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML and CSS on the existing getaimaestro.com site. GitHub Pages serves `main`. This record covers the `/business/` preview only. The public homepage and shared navigation stay as they are.

## Users

Company buyers are the customer. They need to understand the value and forward a useful brief to an implementer. Internal IT, advisers, consultants, and developers are the adoption channel, not the primary reader of this page.

## Product Purpose

AI Maestro coordinates work that company agents already do: a request stays visible, agents continue, a person decides, and a record remains. Success on this page is comprehension and a handoff, not a signup.

## Positioning

Open-source orchestration for company agent teams. The current product is a self-hosted MIT core plus a paid remote companion for a server the company runs. It is not a hosted account.

## Operating Context

A business reader evaluates one bounded workflow, then sends the evaluation brief to IT or an adviser. The implementer continues at aimaestro.sh and the product repository. There is no form on this page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed and current: self-hosted dashboard and orchestration (agents, terminal and chat, tasks, history, memory, multi-machine coordination on infrastructure the company operates). Remote iPhone companion: 30 days from the first connection to the company’s server, then $19.99 once for 1.x. Android is in beta. The companion purchase is not a company subscription.

Planned, not available: a managed company platform, and help implementing AI Maestro on a customer’s own cloud. No signup, partner directory, certification, SSO, RBAC, compliance claim, or SLA.

Agent programs are unequal. Codex has no sidebar “needs you” signal. Pi does not ask before tool calls. Parts of Pi and Copilot support were checked with fixtures or limited sessions. Other terminal agents do not automatically get the same chat, status, and approvals.

Self-hosting is not a promise that all data stays on the machine. Model providers, and memory consolidation when it is on, process what the operator configures. The MIT license, model and provider charges, machines, and the companion purchase are separate costs.

Family and personal offers are a later horizon and are not this page’s job.

## Brand Commitments

The name is AI Maestro. The constellation mark already on the site is the logo. No new trademark.

The user rejected the previous `/business/` design: rust/red, beige/tan, and the serif newspaper treatment. Binding replacement for this surface: Notion-like restraint and clarity, not Notion’s interface or branding. Near-white canvas, black or charcoal type, light neutral dividers, a modern sans for headings and body, minimal color, the current logo as the only mark.

Voice stays concrete. Do not invent customers, metrics, ROI, badges, or guarantees.

## Evidence on Hand

Repository screenshots of the Northwind Labs demo, which uses scripted agents. They prove product views, not customer results. Crops used on this page live in `business/media/`. Technical facts link to aimaestro.sh and the product repository. There are no testimonials or measured business results to show.

## Product Principles

- Name the coordination job before the machinery.
- A business reader should be able to forward the page without learning protocol names first.
- Say what is available, and label what is only planned.
- Show a real product view, and say it is a demo.
- Keep human decisions visible. Do not celebrate bypassing them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The page must work with semantic landmarks, keyboard focus, contrast at WCAG AA, reduced motion, and no horizontal overflow at 360, 390, tablet, and desktop.
